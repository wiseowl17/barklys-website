02 / Mark

The mark

Keep the frame.

White doodle, blue bowtie, gold scallop, script Barkly’s, GROOM · PLAY · STAY. The lockup is the brand — not a clip-art dog.

  • Use the official PNG. Never redraw or recolor.
  • Give it air. Do not crop the gold frame.
  • Do not stretch, outline, or drop the bowtie.
  • Cream or navy fields only. Never on busy photos.
