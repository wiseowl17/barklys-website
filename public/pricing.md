# Pricing — Barkly's

Fear-Free dog grooming, boarding, and daycare in Charlotte, NC. Prices are confirmed at booking; extra time for matting or heavy coat work may be quoted at drop-off.

## Grooming

Full groom includes haircut, bath, dry, ears, and nails.

- Small (0–25 lbs): $75
- Medium (26–40 lbs): $85
- Large (41–70 lbs): $95
- XL (71–90 lbs): $105
- XL+ (91+ lbs): $115
- Touch-up (bath, face, feet & tidy): $10 less than a full groom

## Grooming add-ons

- Deshedding: $25–$40
- Dematting: $20–$40
- Nail trim: $15
- Teeth brushing: $6
- Paw balm: $8
- Specialty shampoo: quoted at drop-off

## Boarding (overnight, in our home)

- Overnight: $50/night
- Holiday rate: $60/night
- Additional dog: +$40/night
- Puppy rate: $55/night
- Bathing/grooming during a stay: +$50 each
- Extended care: 50–100% of nightly rate, confirmed at booking

## Daycare & dog sitting

Daytime only, quoted per visit. Request through the boarding form at https://www.barklysclt.com/boarding#request.
